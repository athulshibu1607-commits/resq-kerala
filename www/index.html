Flood Survival Protocols (വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കം)
Actions during rapid water rise
  • Turn off Main Power: Switch off main MCB before water reaches switchboards to avoid fatal electrocution.
  • Store Potable Water: Fill all clean pots, bottles, and buckets before piped supply becomes contaminated.
  • Move to Upper Floors: Keep torches, battery radios, medicine pouches, and whistle handy. Never enter closed attics without roof hatch.
  • Do Not Walk in Fast Currents: Just 6 inches of moving floodwater can sweep away an adult. Use a sturdy stick to check ground firmness.
⚠️ Disconnect solar inverters and battery backup units if water level reaches room floor.
🐍
Snakebite Protocol (പാമ്പുകടി)
Kerala KSDMA Life-saving steps
  • Immobilize Immediately: Keep victim calm. Any muscle movement accelerates venom circulation into blood stream.
  • Splint the Limb: Bandage limb loosely with wooden splint at heart level (like a bone fracture).
  • DO NOT CUT OR SUCK: Never cut bite site, never attempt to suck venom, never apply ice or electrical shock.
  • NO Tight Tourniquets: Tight rubber tourniquets cause gangrene and limb amputation.
  • Rush to Taluk/Medical College: Administer Anti-Snake Venom (ASV). All Government Taluk Hospitals in Kerala stock ASV 24x7.
📞 Call 108 immediately for anti-venom stocked ambulance dispatch.
🏔️
Landslide Safety (ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ)
High-range safety in Idukki, Wayanad & Ghats
  • Watch Warning Signs: Sudden muddying of stream water, cracking sounds in trees, doors sticking, new ground cracks.
  • Evacuate Perpendicular: Move across the slope, NOT downhill in the flow path of debris and mud.
  • Avoid River Valleys: During continuous heavy rainfall (Red Alert), relocate from hill bases to designated camp schools.
💧
Safe Water & Disease Prevention
Preventing Leptospirosis (എലിപ്പനി) & Cholera
  • Boil for 1 Full Minute: Always bring flood zone water to a rolling boil before drinking.
  • Doxycycline Prophylaxis: Rescue workers and persons stepping into flood water should take Doxycycline 200mg once weekly as advised by Health Dept.
  • Chlorinate Open Wells: Use bleaching powder (5g per 1000 litres) before drawing well water after flood retreat.
